§ I — Saturday School

In the 1980s and 1990s John Hughes became a prolific director creating many famous movies ranging from coming of age films to timeless comedies watched year-round.

One of John Hughes major hits at the time was The Breakfast Club. Portraying a group of teens in Saturday school, this movie accurately captured the state and attitudes of teens living in the 1980s, becoming an instant classic for the generation. Many teens watched this movie and felt that finally their anxieties and issues were taken seriously. Before this, movies depicting teenagers often glanced over the real issues that teens faced.

John Hughes also made popular movies which connected to families in the 80s. One example of this is Uncle Buck which connected with teens and their struggles in high school, along with the worries and struggles parents go through taking care of their kids. He also made The Great Outdoor which became a very popular comedy with many parents in the 80s enjoying its humor.

Planes, Trains, and Automobiles have also become a staple in holiday traditions being watched by many every year during Thanksgiving. The movie is a comedy focusing on a man's journey home for Thanksgiving to see his family. Along his way he faces cancellations, storms, and many other roadblocks which delay his arrival home. For my family, this movie has become a family tradition with it being played at Thanksgiving every year.

§ II — Holiday on Repeat

While John Hughes made major phenomenal movies for his time, John Hughes also made timeless classics that even kids and teens of today's age can relate to.

A good example of this is Home Alone. Home Alone has cemented itself as a timeless Christmas movie with many people watching it as part of their holiday traditions. Focusing on Kevin, a kid forgotten as their family went on vacation, we watch him protecting his house from robbers as they try to break into his house. Kevin uses traps to stop the robbers and save his house. While the original movie is renowned and well known in today's generation, the movie has still had sequels and spinoffs made off of the original premise, with a new rendition even coming out in 2021.

Holiday movies were not his only timeless successes. Another movie series John Hughes directed was National Lampoon's Vacation. While this movie does have holiday connections with the sequel titled Nation Lampoon's Christmas Vacation often being watched during the holidays. The original movie still has cultural relevance in today's world. The movie focuses on a family going on a road trip across the country to visit a theme park. This movie, while old, has had cultural revitalization with a remake coming out in 2015 connecting the original story with modern day youths.
